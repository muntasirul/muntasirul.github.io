Inside the research
Developed a two-stage deep learning pipeline using full-resolution microscopy images. YOLOv8m achieved 0.895 mAP50–95 for blood-cell detection, while Inception-ResNet-v2 achieved 0.9995 accuracy and 0.9953 Macro-F1 for classification.
